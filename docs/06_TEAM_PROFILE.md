## 🏛️ Team Overview & Institutional Identity

The ATLAS-MORPH research team is based at **Nile University of Nigeria, Abuja**, bringing together student researchers in machine learning, distributed systems, and computational linguistics under the mentorship of the Department of Computer Science.

Our mission is to advance sovereign Nigerian AI by solving fundamental hardware and algorithmic bottlenecks, aligning directly with the National Artificial Intelligence Strategy (NAIS).
