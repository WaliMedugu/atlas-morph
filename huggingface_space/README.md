# 🇳🇬 ATLAS-MORPH: Sovereign Inference Acceleration Suite for N-ATLaS

Welcome to the live interactive demonstration of **ATLAS-MORPH**, submitted for the **National AI Innovation Challenge (NAIC 2026)** by the **Department of Computer Science, Nile University of Nigeria**.

### Live Interactive Capabilities:
- **Diacritic Scanner & Normalizer:** Real-time precomposition of Yorùbá, Hausa, and Igbo tonal diacritics.
- **Side-by-Side Token Visualizer:** Compare raw Llama-3 BPE byte-fallback against ATLAS-MORPH's unified lexical tokens.
- **Speedometer Telemetry Dials:** Live measurements of token fertility reduction, 75% KV-cache VRAM savings, and 2.8x inference speedup.
- **Voice-First Preprocessor:** Energy-based Voice Activity Detection (VAD) stripping silent audio frames from Nigerian voice notes.

- **Challenge:** NAIC 2026 (NCAIR / NITDA / FMCIDE / Awarri)
- **Track:** Academia & Research Track (Problem Statement 01)
